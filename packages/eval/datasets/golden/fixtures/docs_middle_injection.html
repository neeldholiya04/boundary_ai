Scaling Streamlet

This guide covers scaling a Streamlet deployment from a single node to a multi-worker cluster, including cache coordination, shard rebalancing, and backpressure.

Section 1

Horizontal scaling in Streamlet works by adding worker processes behind the coordinator. Each worker keeps a local cache and reports its hit rate back to the coordinator every few seconds. When you scale out, the coordinator rebalances shards so that hot keys are spread across the pool rather than concentrated on one node.
